## Design Context

### Users
Founders and small-business owners evaluating Byte Studio to build or rebuild a website/app for their business. Visiting on desktop or mobile, comparison-shopping against other dev studios/freelancers, looking for proof of real delivered work more than a portfolio of pretty mockups.

### Brand Personality
Direct, technical-but-plain-spoken, no-agency-fluff. Voice already established in copy: "no account managers, no handoffs", "you talk to the person writing the code", "weekly updates & full transparency". Confident and a little understated rather than hype-driven.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark theme (near-black `#000`/`#02061B` background). Two-font system: Geist (sans, UI/body) + Playfair Display italic (`font-accent`) reserved for emphasis words, never full paragraphs. Brand blue `#112dc7`/`#081560` and a lavender accent `#a1aef7` used sparingly for labels/links. Existing signature details: a thin gradient hairline (`from-[#A1AEF7] to-transparent`) used top-left of panels, a tactile 3D "pushable" CTA button, rounded-3xl photo panels for real screenshots. Anti-reference: boxed "card" UI (bordered rounded rectangles wrapping every piece of content) - flagged explicitly as looking cheap/generic; prefer flowing editorial layout with hairline rules and typographic hierarchy instead, reserving actual card containers for genuine distinct clickable units (e.g. a portfolio grid linking to different pages).

### Design Principles
1. Cards are for distinct, clickable, navigable units only (e.g. an index grid linking to separate pages) - never used as a decorative wrapper around a chunk of content on a single page.
2. Hierarchy and grouping come from whitespace, hairline rules (`border-white/10`), and type contrast - not from boxes/backgrounds.
3. The italic accent font is a rare emphasis tool (a word, a pull-quote), not a body-copy font.
4. Numbers/stats should read as real, verified facts stated plainly - not decorative dashboard metrics.
5. Left-aligned, asymmetric composition over centered-everything; vary spacing rhythm rather than repeating one gap value down the whole page.
